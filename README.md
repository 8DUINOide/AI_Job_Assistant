# AI Job Assistant

An AI-powered job-hunting assistant designed to streamline the job search process by automating application tracking, job discovery, and resume tailoring. It takes the heavy lifting out of applying for jobs so you can focus on landing interviews.

## Features

- **Automated Job Discovery**: Scrapes job postings from various platforms based on your specific criteria, ensuring you never miss a relevant opportunity.
- **AI-Powered Resume Tailoring**: Automatically adapts your resume to match specific job descriptions, highlighting your most relevant skills to stand out to recruiters.
- **Comprehensive Application Tracking**: Keep track of your job applications, current statuses, deadlines, and follow-ups in a centralized dashboard.
- **Master Profile Management**: Maintain a dynamic master profile of your experiences, skills, and education to quickly build customized resumes.
- **Automated Follow-ups**: Seamlessly prepare and send application or follow-up emails.

## Ecosystem

- **Mobile App**: An Android companion application for on-the-go access to your job hunt pipeline.
- **Browser Extension**: Direct integration with job boards right from your browser, making it easy to save and track jobs.
- **Web Dashboard**: A user-friendly web interface for managing your entire job hunt workflow and profile.

## Getting Started

1. Clone the repository to your local machine.
2. Install the required dependencies:
   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   ```
3. Configure the required environment variables for the necessary services (e.g., Gemini API, Firebase credentials).
4. Launch the application to start accelerating your job search.
